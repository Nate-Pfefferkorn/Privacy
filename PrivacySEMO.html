SEMO Digital Security Guide

Practical security for students, faculty, and staff.

Stop. Verify. Report.

Scammers use email, text, and calls to steal credentials and money, often posing as SEMO IT, professors, or banks.

Warning signs

Urgency and pressure

"Act now," "Account suspended," "Last chance." Legitimate senders don't demand instant action.

Unexpected links or attachments

Hover before clicking. A link that says "semo.edu" may go somewhere else entirely.

Generic or mismatched sender

The display name can say anything. Check the actual email address. Real SEMO staff use @semo.edu.

Gift cards or wire transfers

No legitimate employer, professor, or IT department will ask for payment this way.

What to do

01Don't click links or open attachments. Close the message.
02Open a fresh email or call the sender using a number you already have, not one in the message.
03Forward suspicious messages to SEMO IT at helpdesk@semo.edu or report to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov.

SEMO-specific: employment scams

Fake job offers spike each semester. High pay for little work is a red flag. Never share your SSN or banking details before meeting an employer in person.

Public wi-fi is public.

On open networks, others can intercept unencrypted traffic. Verify the network, then use a VPN.

Risks

Traffic interception

Unencrypted sessions, logins, and form data can be visible to others on the same network.

Fake hotspots

Attackers create networks named "SEMO Free Wi-Fi" or similar. Always confirm the exact name with staff.

What to do

01Confirm the network name and password with a staff member before connecting.
02Use a VPN. Proton VPN offers a free, unlimited, audited no-logs tier. For campus resources, use SEMO GlobalProtect.
03Avoid banking and financial aid portals on public wi-fi. Switch to cellular data if you don't have a VPN.
04Disable auto-join for open networks. Turn off Bluetooth and AirDrop when not in use.

On-campus: use GlobalProtect

To access Banner, shared drives, or restricted SEMO resources from off-campus, connect through the SEMO GlobalProtect VPN first.

Real-sounding can still be fake.

AI voice cloning and deepfake video can impersonate professors, family, or SEMO officials convincingly enough to request urgent action.

How it works

Voice cloning

A few seconds of public audio is enough to clone a voice. Calls can sound exactly like someone you know.

Deepfake video

Look for unnatural blinking, blurred hair edges, waxy skin, or lips that don't quite sync with audio.

Always paired with urgency

Pressure to act fast and keep it secret is the consistent tell, regardless of how real it sounds.

Example

A voicemail from your professor asking you to email your SEMO login so they can submit your grade. Verify by composing a fresh email to their official @semo.edu address.

What to do

01Pause. Refuse any urgent request for money, passwords, or data received through voice or video alone.
02Hang up. Call back on a number you already have saved, not one they provided.
03Set a codeword with family or close contacts for any payment request. A real person will know it.
04Keep social profiles private to reduce the public audio and photos available for scraping.

Quick privacy settings.

High-impact changes you can make right now. Each takes under 30 seconds.

Stop app tracking

Settings / Privacy and Security / Tracking / off

Limit location access

Settings / Privacy and Security / Location Services / set each app to "While Using"

Turn off personalized ads

Settings / Privacy and Security / Apple Advertising / off

Hide lock screen previews

Settings / Notifications / Show Previews / "When Unlocked"

Disable significant locations

Settings / Privacy / Location / System Services / Significant Locations / off

Review permission dashboard

Settings / Security and Privacy / Privacy Dashboard

Limit location per app

Settings / Apps / [App] / Permissions / Location / "Only while using"

Delete advertising ID

Settings / Privacy / Ads / Delete Advertising ID

Revoke camera and mic from unused apps

Settings / Apps / [App] / Permissions / Camera or Microphone / Deny

Auto-reset unused app permissions

Settings / Apps / [App] / Permissions / Allow permission resets / on

Enable MFA everywhere

Use Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator for your SEMO account, bank, and social accounts. App-based MFA stops most account takeovers even when a password is stolen.
